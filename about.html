Who We Are

We design spaces
that tell stories

Habitat Studio is a Nairobi-based interior architecture practice. We believe the spaces you inhabit should reflect who you are — considered, honest, and quietly beautiful.

Our Philosophy

"A room should never be finished — it should feel like it has always been, and still has room to grow."

Every project we take on begins with listening. We want to understand how you move through your day, what textures comfort you, which light makes you feel most at home. Only then do we begin to design.

We draw from both East African craft traditions and contemporary global design — finding the balance between the two that feels right for each client.
